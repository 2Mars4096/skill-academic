# Structure a paper’s introduction

Shared rules · [Academic manuscripts](../academic-writing/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Plan or reorganize an introduction.

**Do** — Give each paragraph a one-sentence purpose. Lead from the substantive question to the empirical obstacle, design, findings, and contribution. Use a short roadmap when it helps navigate the paper.

**Don’t** — Repeat the findings preview in the contribution paragraph.

**Check** — Reading the paragraph purposes in order gives a coherent account of why the study was needed and what it found.

**Stay asleep for** — Write a standalone abstract with a strict word limit.
