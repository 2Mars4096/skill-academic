# Research discovery and design

[Meso-skill map](../README.md) · [Micro-skill category](../../micro-skills/research-design/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Develop alternatives while the research question is still open.

**Suggest** — Use the shortlist when different mechanisms could explain the same observation and the choice would change what to study.

- [Screen research questions](../meso-screen-research-questions/SKILL.md) — Creative comparison.

**Stay asleep for** — A chosen question with a known comparison belongs in test design. A wording edit can proceed directly.

**Stop** — Return at most one suggestion, its expected output, and its pass limit. A category selection opens a menu. Only an explicit choice of a task starts that task.
