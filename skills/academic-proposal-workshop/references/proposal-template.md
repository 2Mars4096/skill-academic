## Drafting Rules

- Keep claims proportional to the actual design.
- Separate `known`, `assumed`, and `needs verification`.
- Do not overstate causality when the evidence plan is descriptive.
- Do not bury data risk. Surface it explicitly.
- End with an execution plan, not just a conclusion.
