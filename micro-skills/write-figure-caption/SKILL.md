# Write figure captions and notes

Shared rules · [Research exhibits and presentations](../research-exhibits/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Write or revise the caption and notes for a research figure.

**Do** — Read the surrounding section and identify the figure’s role there. Name the substantive comparison in the caption and explain only the encodings and qualifications needed to read the figure. Keep notes concise and consistent with the text’s level of technical detail.

**Don’t** — Introduce training or construction machinery in an introductory schematic before it is needed, or turn notes into a repeated methods section.

**Check** — The caption fits its place in the argument and the reader can decode the figure without unexplained notation.

**Stay asleep for** — Change plot geometry or estimate the quantities to be plotted.
