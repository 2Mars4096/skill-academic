# Screen research questions

Shared rules · [Research discovery and design](../research-design/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Narrow a set of early empirical research ideas.

**Do** — State a clear question, the gap in the relevant literature, and the mechanism that could produce a non-obvious answer. Keep alternatives substantively different. In feasibility screening, identify the first data or identification check. In open brainstorming, retain promising ideas whose data needs remain unresolved.

**Don’t** — Stack claims about societal importance in place of causal or economic logic, or make the question more complicated to make it sound academic.

**Check** — The reader can explain why the question is open, how the mechanism works, and what evidence would distinguish the answer.

**Stay asleep for** — Design the test for a question the team has already selected.
