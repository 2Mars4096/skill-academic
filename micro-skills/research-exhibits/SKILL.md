# Research exhibits and presentations

**Use when** — Turn empirical evidence into a table, figure, or talk.

**Do** — Use the same outcome names, units, group labels, and visual conventions across exhibits. Give each exhibit a comparison the audience can identify.

**Don’t** — Repeat the same title or explanation in the graphic, caption, and surrounding text.

**Check** — A reader can move between two exhibits without relearning what a label or color means.

**Stay asleep for** — Discuss a research idea without preparing an exhibit or talk.
