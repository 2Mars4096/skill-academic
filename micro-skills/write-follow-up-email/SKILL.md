# Write a professional follow-up email

Shared rules · [Professional communication and follow-up](../professional-communication/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Follow up after a conversation, introduction, or promised exchange.

**Do** — Identify the connection briefly, deliver what was promised, and preserve the sender’s own intended follow-up. Include relevant current role and affiliation when the recipient needs that context. Use the relationship and tone established in the supplied draft.

**Don’t** — Recap the whole conversation, turn an exchange of ideas into a one-sided request, or shorten away the sender’s relevant identity.

**Check** — The email is sendable, the recipient can place the sender, and the intended next step is clear.

**Stay asleep for** — Draft a one-line scheduling message for chat.
