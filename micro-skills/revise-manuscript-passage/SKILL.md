# Make a targeted manuscript revision

Shared rules · [Academic manuscripts](../academic-writing/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Address specific comments, wording changes, formatting requests, or a request for marked edits.

**Do** — Recover the agreed substantive change from the preceding discussion and apply it to the requested draft. Revise nearby wording when it contradicts that change. When marked edits are requested, show only the changed spans. For a formatting or page-limit pass, begin from the full draft, cut repetition first, and preserve findings and original phrasing as far as the limit permits.

**Don’t** — Treat insertion alone as completing a substantive revision, rewrite findings during a formatting pass, or carry an earlier permission beyond the user’s latest limit on what to edit.

**Check** — The revised passage implements the agreed meaning, its surrounding text is consistent, and any marked comparison shows the actual extent of the edit.

**Stay asleep for** — Draft a new section from scratch.
