# Check a descriptive pattern

[Empirical code and data](../meso-empirical-analysis/SKILL.md) · [Related micro rule](../../micro-skills/empirical-analysis/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — A research question needs its first observable comparison in the data.

**Correction behind the workflow** — An overall mean can hide changing coverage or a handful of extreme values. Counts and denominators help establish whether the proposed pattern is actually present.

**Begin** — Run only after the person selects this task for the current request.

1. Translate the proposed explanation into a first observable comparison. Define the groups or categories and why they correspond to that explanation. Specify the observation unit, time window, and population, and check that counting repeated records will give the intended quantity.
2. Show whether the proposed pattern exists using a compact table or plot with contributing counts. For a share, identify whether its denominator is the matched subset or the full eligible population. Inspect unmatched coverage before changing the population represented by that denominator.
3. Use the displayed comparison to choose its immediate diagnostic. Inspect tails when a mean and median diverge, or changing sample composition when coverage shifts over time. Correct a construction error once and recheck. Report how much of the proposed pattern the evidence actually supports before advancing to a further model.

**Deliver and stop** — Return the exhibit, the size and direction of the observed pattern, and the main limitation. If the data show little difference or give an imprecise picture, report that outcome and stop. Allow at most one targeted correction and recheck. Another round or workflow requires a fresh human choice.

**Stay asleep for** — Fit the first empirical model or design a follow-up for an estimate already in hand.
