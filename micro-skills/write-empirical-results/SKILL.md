# Write an empirical results section

Shared rules · [Academic manuscripts](../academic-writing/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Explain an estimate or sequence of exhibits.

**Do** — Start from the reader’s question after the previous paragraph, explain the comparison that answers it, and give the finding in the right units. Keep the few numbers needed for the argument. Check that cited estimates appear in the retained exhibits or are explicitly requested as untabulated results.

**Don’t** — Reintroduce an omitted result in prose, enumerate every coefficient, or repeat estimator labels and interpretation caveats in each sentence.

**Check** — Each paragraph answers a motivated question, agrees with the retained evidence, and leads naturally to the next test.

**Stay asleep for** — Produce a regression table without writing accompanying prose.
