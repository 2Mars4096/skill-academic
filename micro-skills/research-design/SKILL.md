# Research discovery and design

**Use when** — Choose evidence, questions, or designs for an empirical study.

**Do** — Start from the phenomenon or decision the study would explain. Identify what could be observed and what comparison would be informative.

**Don’t** — Treat a broad topic or a list of available datasets as a research question.

**Check** — A reader can name the question and the piece of evidence that would move it forward.

**Stay asleep for** — Polish the wording of a research question the author has already chosen.
