# Review a paper as a referee

Shared rules · [Academic manuscripts](../academic-writing/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Write an independent referee report on an empirical paper.

**Do** — Read the submitted paper on its own terms. Judge the contribution, measurement, and identification against the intended journal. Anchor each major concern to a claim or exhibit and explain what evidence could resolve it.

**Don’t** — Import private project history into an independent assessment or present a speculative concern as an established flaw.

**Check** — The author can locate the disputed claim, understand its consequence, and see a way to address it.

**Stay asleep for** — Draft the authors’ response to a referee.
