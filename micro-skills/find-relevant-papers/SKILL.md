# Find relevant papers

Shared rules · [Research discovery and design](../research-design/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Search for empirical papers that inform a particular question.

**Do** — Use the requested field, venue, paper type, and recency to shortlist sources. Read enough of each original paper to identify the problem it solves and the implemented step it supports. For methodological searches, distinguish direct precedents from adaptations and analogies.

**Don’t** — Present a famous or top-journal citation as support for an exact procedure it does not implement.

**Check** — Each shortlisted paper has a verifiable reference and a precise account of what can be followed and what would require adaptation.

**Stay asleep for** — Find a dataset with the fields needed for a proposed regression.
