# Write a paper’s data section

[Academic manuscripts](../meso-academic-writing/SKILL.md) · [Related micro rule](../../micro-skills/write-data-section/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Draft or substantially revise the section that connects raw records to the estimation sample.

**Correction behind the workflow** — Readers need to know what the sources contain before they encounter the restrictions. A sample count is meaningful only once the unit and construction are clear.

**Begin** — Run only after the person selects this task for the current request. A suggestion alone ends at the suggestion.

1. Trace the available source documentation and construction code or notes. Identify source coverage, what one raw record represents, linkage or aggregation, sample restrictions, the final observation unit, and the definitions used in estimation.
2. Draft in the order the reader needs. Introduce the sources, explain how the records become eligible observations, then give supported sample counts. Define a measure where it first matters. Leave extended construction detail in its established location.
3. Trace one eligible observation through the prose. Compare its unit, eligibility, denominator, and reported sample size with the construction evidence. If the trace fails, revise the affected passage once and repeat that check.

**Deliver and stop** — Deliver the section. For any missing count or undocumented restriction, identify the precise fact requiring confirmation. End the pass there. This run permits one initial pass and at most one targeted correction and recheck. A further round or another workflow needs a fresh human choice.

**Stay asleep for** — Decide whether to acquire a dataset, or change the wording of one sentence.
