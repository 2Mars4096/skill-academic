# Summarize a meeting and its actions

Shared rules · [Professional communication and follow-up](../professional-communication/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Turn notes or a transcript into preparation for the next meeting.

**Do** — Group related points by subject. For each, distinguish what was discussed or decided from what needs doing next. Include an owner or deadline when the discussion establishes one.

**Don’t** — Turn every idea raised into an agreed task or present an inferred remark as a quotation.

**Check** — The reader can use the summary to prepare for the next meeting and trace each action to the discussion.

**Stay asleep for** — Report what has been implemented since a feedback round.
