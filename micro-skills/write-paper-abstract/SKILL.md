# Write a paper’s abstract

Shared rules · [Academic manuscripts](../academic-writing/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Compress an empirical paper into a standalone abstract.

**Do** — Lead with the substantive question, identify the setting and comparison, then state the principal findings and what they establish. Give the most informative magnitude when space allows.

**Don’t** — Spend the closing sentence on a generic caveat, a list of secondary outcomes, or a claim broader than the evidence.

**Check** — A reader can say what was studied, how it was tested, and what was learned without consulting the introduction.

**Stay asleep for** — Outline the paragraph sequence of the full introduction.
