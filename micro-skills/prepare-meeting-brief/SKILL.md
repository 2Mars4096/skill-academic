# Prepare a research meeting brief

Shared rules · [Professional communication and follow-up](../professional-communication/SKILL.md)

**Use when** — Prepare concise research material for an upcoming discussion.

**Do** — Use the established meeting format and the selected questions from the previous discussion. Explain the actual rule, comparison, or decision needed for each chosen topic, with enough detail to discuss it. Keep an explicit length target in view.

**Don’t** — Cover every past topic by default, add a formal abstract, or describe a process without saying how assignments or choices are made.

**Check** — A collaborator can read the brief quickly and discuss the substantive decisions without reconstructing the prior conversation.

**Stay asleep for** — Extract a complete record of decisions and actions from a finished meeting.
