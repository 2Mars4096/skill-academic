# Professional communication and follow-up

**Use when** — Turn supplied facts, discussion, or feedback into a message or follow-up.

**Do** — Write for the recipient’s next action. Keep relevant context, the sender’s current position or progress when it matters, and the actual ask.

**Don’t** — Replay a whole conversation or invent a commitment, deadline, or decision.

**Check** — The recipient can tell what matters now and whether a reply or action is needed.

**Stay asleep for** — Revise a paper or interface without preparing communication for a recipient.
